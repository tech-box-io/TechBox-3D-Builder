stated for deconstruction guides, and every in-house practice the MRA's and RCC's deconstructions follow, each with its source and
how it applies to the LSM. Read-only study: nothing was run, regenerated or changed (only this file was written).

used for the MRA. Remembering some things can be removed in groups (like the 3 breadboard wires, etc.)"

## B. Order and granularity

| # | Rule | Source | For the LSM |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | As a first draft, the Full Build in reverse; the order is near-reverse, not exact (the published take-aparts go by physical access: housing in reverse, electronics component by component, plug-in parts last). | rulebook 241, 245, 247 - 248, 256; lee_notes_2026-10-18.md:10; MRA decon.py 3 - 6 | The Full Build's reverse runs: stems (40, 39), D8, D7 (38, 37), D11 x4 (36 - 34), D6 (33), matrix + D10 out (32, 31), matrix back out over D4 (30), D9s (29), D5, D3 (28, 27, turned over), matrix back over D4 (26), D4 (25), D2 (24), USB (23), button pins (22, 21), nut / button / D2 in the hand (20, 19), sockets off the matrix (17 - 15), pins out of the breadboard (13 - 11), R330 (9), capacitor (7), R12K (5), Nano (3). Which of the two Lee wants for the matrix is a decision (I below). |
| B2 | Parts may go in unison: several units in one step, each its own way (beyond the published guides' identical sets; the generator notes more than two arrows). | Lee 17 Oct, rulebook 248 - 250; stepgen 1134 - 1135 | Lee named "the 3 breadboard wires, etc." Natural groups: the 64 stems in one step (the published Step 1; the build's 39 + 40 merged, "one, then the rest" merges, rulebook 242); the two D9s (one step in the build and the published guide); the four D11 posts (2 + 2 in the build and the published guide, all four together in the build's Step 36: either way); the three jumper wires (pins out of i2, i3, b19 together; sockets off V+, IN, V- together, or both ends at once); the button's two pins out of j5 and j16 together. The small plug-ins (R330, capacitor, R12K, Nano) are one per step in the build and in the published take-apart; keep them so unless Lee says. |
| B3 | Wires that went on one at a time may come off together: the MRA's eight breadboard wires came off the joysticks and the module at once (both ends in one step); the phased alternative (joysticks pulled off the wires, then the wires off the module) was offered, not done. Granularity default otherwise: per module bundle. | lee_notes_2026-10-18.md:11; rulebook 256 - 257; rulebook 325 | The LSM's three jumper wires are such a bundle. Both ends at once is the MRA's form; the published LSM does pins at the breadboard first (one per step), then the 3-wire connector off the matrix in the hand. |
| B4 | A part once off is gone for good: removed, never reconnected; nothing is refitted for storage. | Lee 17 Oct, rulebook 250 - 251; stepgen 904; runthrough.js 41 | The nut is not put back on the button; D10 not put back on the matrix. |
| B5 | A lead unplugged at one end dangles from its other end until that end's part goes (in-house; the published guides do the same). Still open: whether "disconnected" means each plug or fully free. A lead with one end on screen when its other end's part is removed is refused. | rulebook 251 - 252; stepgen 909 - 910, 1100 - 1112; CLAUDE.md 340 - 341 | E.g. unplug the three pins at the breadboard and the wires dangle from the matrix (sockets riding on MATRIX) until the sockets come off; unplug the button's pins and they dangle from BUTTON until the button goes. Removing MATRIX (its sockets ride on it) while the pins are still in the breadboard is refused: unplug first, or take both ends together. |
| B6 | Wired modules are lifted off the housing first, still wired, and unplugged later (`lift`). | rulebook 239; CLAUDE.md 360 - 364 | The matrix (with D10 and its sockets) out of the box still wired to the breadboard; D2 with the button stood back out at D2_OUT (guide.py 559 - 560) still wired, as the Full Build had it before Step 24. |
| B7 | Leads are unplugged before the structure around them comes out, as the build plugged them in last (the RCC's wheel motors before L6, L7 and the module). Read with B1: the reverse of the build. | rulebook 280 - 281 (Lee 18 Oct, RCC); RCC decon.py 98 - 100 | The LSM build plugs everything in before the walls, so the reverse unplugs after the walls are off: no conflict. |
| B8 | Once a cover is off, electronics come out where they are, before the housing round them (MRA, Lee's first round: B8 off, the wires, the module, the Nano before the joysticks' walls). | rulebook 271 - 274; lee_notes_2026-10-18.md:39; MRA decon.py 232 - 242 | Supports taking the matrix and wires out once D6 is off, as the published LSM does. |
| B9 | A sub-assembly that comes off is lifted off the way it went on, then taken apart on its own (`alone=`, the rest hidden, close in), then what is left of it is `put_away()` and the guide returns to the main model. | rulebook 239, 277 - 280; MRA decon.py 116 - 121, 150 - 173, 181 - 185, 199 - 203, 215 - 230, 240 - 254; RCC decon.py 68 - 87, 92 - 96, 106 - 112, 117 - 132; CLAUDE.md 365 | The LSM's two: D2 with the push button (nut, then button; then put_away D2) and the LED matrix (D10, the sockets; then put_away MATRIX), as the published take-apart (Steps 14 - 15, 18 - 19). | B10 | Leads of a module handled on its own are drawn cut short, fading out over their last 30 mm (Lee 13/14 Oct, build rule); in a deconstruction `wires_here={id: route}` draws them so on that step only. | rulebook 334; stepgen 921 - 922; CLAUDE.md 345 | The matrix in the hand while its wires' pins are still in the breadboard (hidden), if that order is chosen. |
| B11 | A fastener: in the published guides every fastener gets its own step off. | B12 | The nut turns as it comes off: one or two whole turns over the move, clockwise seen from the head when unscrewing, a quarter turn a pose so a hexagon is never seen turning backwards (Lee 19 Oct). | rulebook 88; guide.py 528 - 541 (`nut_turns`) and 553 - 554 (`NUT_ON`) | Give the nut's way by hand (`path=`), e.g. the reverse of `NUT_ON` (two turns over two beats, unscrewing). | B13 | A two-stage joint is undone inside one step (small unhook or tilt first, then the long pull); the generator does this by itself from the Full Build's way, its stops kept (MRA's C4 / C5: slid 5 mm along C3, then pulled out). | rulebook 237; lee_notes_2026-10-19.md:26; stepgen 1003 - 1021 | D3 (hooked into D4, swung shut: Step 27), D4 (lowered beside D1, then pushed on: 25), the matrix (lifted over, lowered: 32 with `via`), D10 (held under, slid along: 31, but see F3). |

## C. Cues: arrows, eyes, close-ups, labels

| # | Rule | Source | For the LSM |
|---|---|---|---|

## F. Generator behaviour that touches these conventions (found while checking them, for the planner)

1. **D11_A - D11_D ride on D1** (Completed View carry; guide.py 803 - 813: they come in by `appear` + `ride`, never in `moving`), so the
   Full Build recorded no way in for them (stepgen 847 - 858 reads `moving` only). `remove(D11s)` would fall to "not in its seat:
   lifted straight up, unchecked" with a Review note (stepgen 1027 - 1032). Give `direction=[0, 1, 0]` and `hover=` (or `path=`).
