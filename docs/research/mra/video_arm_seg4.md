## What this stretch is, in one paragraph

One thing is built: the triangle linkage on the +Z side of the arm. The triangle A10 gets link A11 and link A12 screwed to it in the
hand; the model is turned round; the free end of A11 is screwed to the lobe of side plate R6 from outside; the model is laid on its
side; the triangle's last hole and strip A13 are screwed to the top (elbow) end of upper-arm bar A8. Four screws go in, all of them
pivots, all with their heads on the +Z side. No servo, horn or wire is touched and nothing is plugged in. No servo is centred or
powered. The video ends with A12 and A13 (and the forearm on the other side) hanging free, waiting for the claw.

## 4. Hard to show in a 3D guide, or where video and CAD may not match

1. **Built in the hand first.** A10 + A11 + A12 are joined away from the model, held on edge, and only then taken to the turret. A
   guide that adds parts to the model one at a time in their CAD places cannot show this as filmed; it needs either a "build this
   group on the table" passage or a different order (the CAD allows A11 behind R6 first, then A10, then A12).
2. **The order differs from the CAD-derived order in `cad_arm_claw.md` section 7.4.** There: linkage lowered in, A13 (CAD A10), elbow
   screw NAUO71, and last the turret screw NAUO70. On video: turret screw NAUO70 FIRST, then A13 and the elbow screw. The video order
   lets the linkage hang from the turret while the elbow is lined up.
3. **The model is turned and laid over.** Controller at the top for steps 1-5, turned 180 degrees for 6-10, laid on its -Z side for
   11-14 so the elbow screw can be dropped in and driven downwards. In the guide this is a camera move to the +Z side; the house rule
   "camera fixed inside a Step N / Step Nc pair" still holds, but the +Z view is a new camera for this group of steps.
4. **A floppy linkage.** After NAUO70 the triangle hangs down from A11 (10:48); the CAD shows it at one fixed attitude. Likewise A12 and
   A13 hang or swing until the claw is fitted. The guide will show CAD-pose angles that the real parts do not hold by themselves.
5. **Hidden hole at the turret.** A11's end is behind R6; the student sees only R6's hole. A lead line has to run from the screw to
   the hole in R6's lobe, and the caption has to say that A11 goes INSIDE R6.
6. **Three layers at the elbow.** NAUO71 passes through A13 and A10 and bites only in A8; A8's hole is hidden under the other two and
   was lined up by feel, which took from 10:56 to 11:15. The CAD has a 0.76 mm air gap between A10 and A8 there; on video A10 is simply
   pressed against A8.
7. **Which way round each strip goes.** A11, A12 and A13 are the same 90 x 10 mm strip outline. What tells them apart on video: the code,
   the triangle (A12), the square (A13), and on A11 nothing but the code. In each case the end WITH the code (and mark) goes on the
   triangle A10 / the elbow. A11 matters most: per the CAD its coded end has the 2.8 mm free hole and its plain end the 2.3 mm threaded
   hole, so reversed it would not work as drawn. The marks are real cut-outs in the CAD (4.0 mm triangle in NAUO26, 3.3 mm triangle in
   NAUO27, 2.6 mm square in NAUO28), so `model.glb` must keep them and the camera must be close enough to show them.
8. **"Do not over-tighten" cannot be seen.** These are pivots, but the builder just drives each screw until it stops turning easily.
   The guide needs words for this; the video gives no visible cue.
9. **Screw lengths are not readable in the video.** Three short screws and one that takes twice as long to drive is all that can be
   seen. M3x6, M3x6, M3x6, M3x10 comes from the CAD.
10. **Servo leads.** Loose on the worktop here; routed through the hook slots and plugged into rows 6 and 9 in the CAD. If the guide
    follows the videos, the two side-servo plug steps cannot come from Arm.mov.
11. **The servo label.** On video each side servo shows a pink label with a plain white sticker across it on the face turned towards
    the camera (the -Z servo's upper face at 10:17, the +Z servo at 11:55). Worth a glance against where the CAD puts the label plate;
    I did not check this further.
12. **A video cut** at 10:16 / 10:17 hides how the model was turned; nothing else happens across it (same parts in the same places).
