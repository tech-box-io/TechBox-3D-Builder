Not in the Full Build, so decided here (say if any should change):
- The I/O module is set down on the table in front of the base (where the Full Build had it while the controller was built), and
  each joystick, once out of its walls, is laid on the table beside it (the joysticks' wires stay on), so that B3, B4 and the base
  can come apart (Steps 52, 57, 61). In the build the joysticks were never on the table at that stage.
- The last T1 and the bare C3, A10, A4, A1 and A6 leave the screen when their sub-assembly is taken apart (as the bench test is put
  away in the build); R1, the last part of the base, gets a step of its own (Step 73).
- B6 off each joystick (Steps 56, 60) is pulled away from the joystick, the reverse of the joystick going in.

Checked: `_runthrough()` on the deconstruction (154 views), the MRA Full Build (211) and the RCC Full Build (61): 0 problems each, with
new checks that a part taken off does not fade while sliding, is half faded half-way through its fade, and fades in again on Prev.
