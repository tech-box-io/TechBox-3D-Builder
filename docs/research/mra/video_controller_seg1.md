# Controller.mov, 0:00 - 2:20 (segment 1 of the controller video)

Status: COMPLETE for 0:00 - 2:23 (plus a look at 2:24 - 2:35 to see where the stretch hands over).

How it was studied: overview at 2 s spacing (72 frames, nine 4 x 2 sheets, all read), then every second of the
stretch re-extracted at the video's native size (1080 x 1920; the frames tool cannot go higher than the source)
and read as 1 s montages, with half-second frames and enlarged crops wherever a code, legend or wire had to be
read. Timestamps are the video's own. "Sure" means the code or detail was read directly in a frame.

Viewpoint: the phone looks down on the worktop from the controller end of the base plate toward the turret. In
assembly space that is from -X looking toward +X, so the builder's left is -Z and the builder's right is +Z.
Checked against the CAD: the "B2" engraving (NAUO2, top face, X -14.9..-5.4, Z 96.8..103.2) is at the builder's
near-left corner in the video, and B5 (NAUO4, Z 105.1..108.1) is the left outer wall.

Short answer to the questions asked about this video:

- No servo lead is plugged into the shield anywhere in 0:00 - 2:35. The only servo lead in play is the base
  servo's, flag "5"; it is moved out of the way twice and left lying off the right side of the plate.
- The I/O shield is NOT slid into place in this stretch. It lies flat on the worktop to the builder's left of the
  base plate the whole time the joystick walls are built, hanging on its two ribbons. It is picked up at 2:20 and
  is still only being held over the left edge of the plate at 2:35.
- No screws are used, no servo horn is touched, no servo is centred or tested in this stretch.

## 6. Things that would be hard to show in a 3D guide, or that may not match the CAD

1. **The joysticks never appear on their own.** They arrive tied to the shield by the two ribbons, so from the
   first joystick step the shield and eight wires have to be somewhere in the picture, lying beside the base. The
   present guide brings the wires in only with the shield step (`appear_after=JOY_WIRES` on the module step),
   which is the reverse of what is on the bench. The CAD ribbon paths (NAUO111 and NAUO112) are drawn in their
   final routed position; during the wall-building steps the real ribbons arch off the left side of the plate.
2. **Joystick into a wall is a sideways push, done in the hand.** The PCB's side edge passes through the wall
   (slots in the two uprights plus the U cut-out), so the motion is along Z, square to the wall face, with the
   joystick coming from the un-engraved side for B5 and for the right-hand B6. This agrees with the note in
   guide.py that the board slides in edge-first. The second wall of each pair is then pushed on along the same
   axis. Each pair is assembled off the base and only then seated, as the guide already has it.
3. **Seating a group is not one clean drop.** It goes down joystick end first over B3, then the arms are
   levelled onto B4, and the hooked post at the far end of each B6 needs its own firm press into the slot near
   the turret. In the CAD B3 and both ends of B6 reach Y 87.1, below B2, so these are snap fits through B2 into
   B1. A straight -Y move is a fair simplification; a separate "press the hook in" cue might be worth having.
4. **B7's engraving faces out on video, in in the CAD.** CAD: NAUO7's engraving is cut into its -Z face (toward
   the right joystick). Video: "B7" is on the outer face (+Z). B7 is symmetrical left to right, so it fits either
   way round. B5 (engraving out, -Z face), the right-hand B6 (engraving on its -Z face, away from its joystick),
   B3 and B4 (engraving toward the builder) were all seen to match the CAD. The left-hand B6 was not seen from
   the side once fitted, but B6 has the long arm on one end and so only goes on one way.
5. **Base servo lead.** It is only moved out of the way (left at 0:08, right at 1:07) and is not routed through
   R4's L-shaped slot or plugged in during this stretch. A 3D guide either has to show a loose lead flopping
   about or leave the lead out until the wiring steps.
6. **Shield orientation at hand-over.** At 2:20 - 2:35 the shield is held on the LEFT of the plate with its USB
   end toward the turret. In the CAD its USB end points to +Z (builder's right) and the analog block faces the
   turret: a quarter turn clockwise, seen from above, from how it is held. If the kit is built as the CAD shows,
   the board has to be turned before it goes in; whether it is, and from which side it enters, is in the next
   stretch. From the CAD: the board (53.3 mm in X) sits in end slots of the two B6 arms
   (X 43.4..45 and X 95..96.7) and on B4, so it can only enter along Z.
7. **Turret turned by hand at 0:02 - 0:07.** Back-driving the base servo is not something the guide should
   teach; it is housekeeping before the build and can be left out.
8. **Order check against the present guide.** Video: B3, B4, left joystick into B5, B6, seat, right joystick into
   B6, B7, seat. The guide has the same order. The video does not fit B8 or the shield before 2:35.
